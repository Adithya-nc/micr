# Refund Policy

POL-001 permits a synthetic autonomous refund at or below 5000 INR only with successful payment, failed or missing order, fresh authoritative evidence, matching identity and amount, and no material contradiction.

POL-002 requires approval above 5000 INR. POL-004 blocks autonomous action on material contradiction. POL-005 requires authoritative evidence. POL-006 escalates after two failed verifications. POL-007 rejects customer statement alone as financial authorization.
